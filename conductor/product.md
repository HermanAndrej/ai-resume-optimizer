# Product Definition

## Project Name

ai-resume-optimizer

## Description

An AI-powered tool that analyzes and optimizes resumes for job applications.

## Problem Statement

Job seekers struggle to tailor resumes to specific job descriptions without knowing what recruiters and ATS systems actually look for. This app helps job seekers tailor their resumes to specific job descriptions, instantly shows ATS compatibility with keyword-gap analysis, and provides concrete feedback on what to improve before applying.

## Target Users

Job seekers in **IT, software engineering, and AI/ML roles** actively applying for positions. The tool is specialized for the tech hiring landscape — SWE, backend, full-stack, AI Engineer, ML Engineer, and adjacent technical roles. It is not designed for non-technical resumes.

### User sub-segments
- **SWE / Backend / Full-stack**: generalist engineers at junior through staff level
- **AI Engineers**: LLM application builders (RAG, agents, prompt pipelines, evals)
- **ML Engineers**: model training, fine-tuning, serving, and MLOps focus
- **Career changers into tech**: bootcamp grads, pivots from adjacent fields

## Key Goals

### 1. Enable honest, grounded resume tailoring
Let job seekers tailor resumes to specific job descriptions by reordering, potentially adding, emphasizing, and omitting profile content — but never fabricating. Every generated resume is validated to ensure it stays truthful.

### 2. Provide visibility into job fit and ATS compatibility
Show candidates upfront what keywords/skills from the JD match their profile, what gaps exist, and how well their tailored resume will parse in applicant tracking systems before they hit apply.

### 3. Maintain a single authoritative profile with clean versioning
Eliminate resume file fragmentation. Manage one profile once, generate unlimited job-specific tailored versions, and preserve full version history so nothing is ever lost.
